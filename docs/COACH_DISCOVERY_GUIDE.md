# GameIQ — Coach Discovery Interview Guide

**Purpose:** A structured interview guide for founder-led discovery conversations with coaches, analysts, and program directors.  
**Format:** 20–30 minute conversation, semi-structured.  
**Goal:** Validate the problem, understand the workflow, test the product reaction, and surface real pricing signals.

---

## Before the Interview

- Ask the interviewee for 20–30 minutes
- Tell them: "I'm building a film review tool for coaches and I want to understand how you work before showing you anything."
- Do not demo the product until after the discovery questions — you want uncontaminated workflow context
- Take notes in a shared doc; do not rely on memory

---

## Opening (2–3 minutes)

Introduce yourself and the project briefly:

> "I'm building a tool called GameIQ — it's an AI-powered coaching report tool. Before I show you anything, I want to understand how you and your staff currently handle film review. You can help me the most by just telling me how things actually work."

Warm-up question:
> "How long have you been coaching, and what level do you work at right now?"

---

## Problem Discovery (7–10 minutes)

### Film review workflow

- **"How do you review game film today — walk me through your process."**
  - Who does it? Just you, or does staff help?
  - Do you use a platform (Hudl, Veo, Pixellot)?
  - How long does the full review take for one game?

- **"How long does it take to go from the final whistle to having player feedback ready?"**
  - Is that too long? What would "fast enough" look like?

- **"What do you actually produce at the end of a film review session?"**
  - Notes? Video clips? A presentation? A document?
  - What format does feedback go to players in?

### Pain points

- **"What's the hardest part of the film review process for you personally?"**
  - Time? Organization? Getting the feedback to players clearly?

- **"What feedback do players actually receive after a game?"**
  - How specific is it? Do players find it useful?
  - Have you ever had a player push back on feedback because it wasn't specific?

- **"Do you ever feel like you're doing the same review tasks over and over without getting faster?"**

- **"What would you spend those hours on if film review took half the time?"**

---

## Current Tools (3–4 minutes)

- **"What tools do you currently use for video — Hudl, Veo, something else?"**
  - What do you like about it? What frustrates you?
  - How much does it cost? Is the team paying, or the program?

- **"Does your current tool produce any reports or structured outputs automatically?"**
  - Or does all the analysis happen manually?

- **"If you could keep one thing about your current workflow and change everything else, what would you keep?"**

---

## Product Reaction (7–10 minutes)

*Now show GameIQ — either the demo workspace or the landing page.*

- **"Before I explain anything — what do you think this does?"**
  - Listen for their mental model before explaining anything

- **"What part of this feels most like your actual workflow?"**

- **"What part felt unnecessary or wrong?"**

- **"If you imagine using this after your next game — what would you do first?"**

- **"What's the one thing that would need to change for you to use this every week?"**

- **"What would you call these tagged moments? We call them 'key moments' — is that the right word?"**

- **"The AI shows a confidence score on each insight — High, Medium, Low. Does that help or is it noise?"**

---

## Willingness to Pay (3–4 minutes)

*Only ask this after the product reaction section — do not lead with pricing.*

- **"If this saved you 90 minutes per game — what would that be worth to your program?"**

- **"What do you pay for Hudl / Veo / your current tool?"**
  - (Do not reveal our pricing first — let them anchor)

- **"If GameIQ cost $X per month for your full coaching staff — would that be a budget conversation, or a program decision?"**
  - Start high and let them push back. $100/month, then adjust.

- **"Is video analysis software something you typically pay for individually, or does the program or club pay?"**

---

## Closing (2 minutes)

Ask:

- **"What's the one question I didn't ask that I should have?"**

- **"Is there another coach or analyst who thinks about this differently from you? Would you be willing to introduce me?"**

Offer:
- Early access if they want to try it with their own team
- Follow-up if they want to be involved in shaping the product

Thank them genuinely:
> "This was exactly the kind of conversation that shapes what we build. I'll follow up if we add something based on what you said."

---

## Interview Ethics

- Do not promise features you have not built
- Do not imply current users or customers if none exist yet
- Be honest that this is an MVP in early access
- Do not record without consent
- Thank interviewees for their time — never ghost after a follow-up promise
