# GameIQ — Landing Page Strategy

**Last updated:** 2026-06-01 — Prompt 17

---

## 1. Target Audience

**Primary:**
- Head coaches at competitive college, club, semi-pro, and academy programs
- Assistant coaches and analysts who handle film review tasks

**Secondary:**
- Sports program directors evaluating tools for their coaching staff
- Early advisors and investors evaluating the product and market

**Not primary:**
- General sports fans
- Recreational recreational leagues
- Individual players without a coach context

---

## 2. Positioning

**One sentence:**
> "GameIQ turns game film into coach-ready intelligence — in minutes, not hours."

**Expanded:**
GameIQ is a structured AI coaching report tool. It takes what coaches already do — watch film, tag events, take notes — and transforms it into evidence-linked insights, player feedback, and a next-practice plan. The product is not a video storage platform and is not a generic "AI sports analytics" dashboard.

**Founder-grade differentiator:**
> "GameIQ does not just store game film. It helps turn film into decisions."

---

## 3. Headline Decision

**Selected headline:**
> "Turn game film into coach-ready intelligence."

**Why this headline:**
- Direct value statement — coaches understand it in 5 seconds
- "Coach-ready" signals the output is usable, not raw
- "Intelligence" sets the tone without overselling automation
- No buzzwords, no vague AI promises

**Subheadline:**
> "GameIQ helps coaches turn match and practice video, tagged key moments, roster data, and notes into evidence-linked reports, player feedback, and next-practice plans."

---

## 5. Copy Principles

### Use:
- "Evidence-linked" — every insight points to a real tagged moment
- "Coach-ready" — the output is immediately usable
- "Tagged key moments" — specific language, not vague "AI analysis"
- "Human-in-the-loop" — coach verification is a feature, not a caveat
- "Confidence scores" — specific, honest, differentiating
- "Practice plan" — coaches understand this immediately
- "Assumptions and limitations" — this builds trust

### Avoid:
- "Fully automated video analysis" — not true in v1
- "AI-powered platform" — generic, no differentiation
- "Replace your coaching staff" — never
- "Guaranteed wins" or "injury prediction" — not built
- "Real-time" — not applicable to v1
- Fake customer logos or testimonials

## 6. CTAs

**Primary CTA:**
> "Try Demo"

- Goes to `/demo` which explains what the demo includes before requiring a signup
- Then links to `/demo/setup` for authenticated users

**Secondary CTA:**
> "Request Access"

- Goes to `/request-access`
- Captures structured interest with role, sport, pain point

**Authenticated users also see:**
> "Go to Dashboard"

---

## 7. MVP Honesty Section

**Why it exists:**
Coaches are skeptical of AI tools. Many have been burned by products that overpromised automated analysis and underdelivered. GameIQ builds trust by being explicit about what v1 does and does not do.

**What to say:**
> "GameIQ's first MVP uses uploaded video, coach notes, roster data, and manually tagged key moments. Full automated player/ball tracking is part of the future roadmap."

**What not to do:**
- Do not bury this in fine print
- Do not apologize for the MVP — frame it as intentional
- The right coach will appreciate the honesty more than a product that hides its limitations

## 9. Design Notes

**Aesthetic:** Dark, premium, athletic but not gimmicky. Clean readability.

**Color system:** Slate-950 background, sky-500 for primary actions, amber for caution/honesty, emerald for trust/positive signals.

**Avoid:**
- Neon sports betting aesthetic
- Fake dashboard screenshots with placeholder data
- Unnecessary animations that hurt readability
- Charts with no real data
